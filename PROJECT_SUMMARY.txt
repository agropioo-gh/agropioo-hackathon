 SECTION 1: WHAT THE PROJECT DOES
================================================================================

Agropioo is a full-stack, AI-powered smart agriculture platform that gives
Pakistani farmers a personal AI advisor, digital farm records, real-time
market intelligence, weather advisories, crop disease diagnosis, pest
outbreak prediction, and profit/loss tracking — all in their own language.

The platform solves one core problem: Pakistani farmers make 60+ critical
decisions per season (when to sow, what fertilizer, which pesticide, when
to irrigate, when to sell) with zero digital tools and zero expert access.

Agropioo answers: "What to do, when to do it" — in the language you speak.


Key Capabilities:
-----------------

1. AI MULTI-AGENT ADVISOR
   - 5 specialist AI agents (Crop, Soil, Pest, Market, Schemes) that
     collaborate to answer any farming question
   - RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) with 21 Pakistan-specific
     agricultural knowledge documents embedded as vectors
   - Conversation memory with summaries for context-aware responses
   - Input/output guardrails keep AI on-topic (farming only)
   - Works in 8 languages: English, Urdu, Punjabi, Pashto, Sindhi,
     Saraiki, Balochi, Hindko

2. AI CROP DISEASE DETECTION
   - Farmer uploads photo of diseased plant/leaf
   - TensorFlow.js model classifies disease from 38+ disease classes
   - Returns: disease name, confidence score, severity level, causes,
     step-by-step treatment plan, rescan timing
   - Images stored on Cloudinary with automatic optimization
   - Full scan history with follow-up chat per diagnosis
   - Works offline — photos queue in IndexedDB, process when connected

3. SMART WEATHER ADVISORY
   - 5-day forecasts from OpenWeather API
   - Farm-specific advisories based on crop type, growth stage, soil
     type, and irrigation method
   - Alert system: heavy rain, frost, extreme heat, disease risk
   - Growth-stage-aware: "Your wheat is at tillering stage — delay
     irrigation by 2 days if frost expected"
   - Acknowledge flow for accountability

4. MANDI PRICE TRACKER & PREDICTOR
   - Live prices for 40+ crops across 200+ agricultural markets
   - 14-day price forecasts with SELL/HOLD recommendations
   - Automated scraping from 5 government sources:
     * amis.pk (Punjab Agriculture Information Service)
     * samis.pk (Sindh)
     * fmis.kp (Khyber Pakhtunkhwa)
     * bmis_balochistan (Balochistan)
     * pbs_spi (Pakistan Bureau of Statistics)
   - Price alerts when market hits target price
   - Favorites system for quick access

5. CROP RECOMMENDATION ENGINE
   - Multi-factor AI scoring: soil type, season, budget, irrigation,
     weather patterns, market prices
   - 40+ Pakistani crops in database with yield/duration/capital data
   - Soil compatibility matrix for 16 districts
   - Crop rotation rules: "After wheat, plant gram — avoid cotton
     for 2 years"
   - 5-dimension scoring (0-100): Soil(25) + Season(25) + Budget(20)
     + Irrigation(15) + Market/Weather(15)

6. PROFIT/LOSS CALCULATOR
   - Create seasons per crop, per year
   - Track expenses: seed, fertilizer, labor, irrigation, transport
   - Projected vs actual cost comparison
   - Break-even analysis with visual charts
   - ROI calculation per season and across years

7. AI PEST OUTBREAK PREDICTION
   - Daily risk scores (0-100) via cron job
   - Considers weather patterns, historical incidence, farm crop type
   - Pest alerts with severity (warning/critical)
   - Escalation tracking for worsening outbreaks
   - Treatment catalog: chemical + organic options with prices

8. APP-CONTROL CHAT AGENT
   - Universal remote for the entire platform
   - Floating chat on every page of the farmer app
   - 12 tools: create/update/delete records, navigate, weather/prices/
     P&L summaries, handoff to AI Advisor, attachments, confirmations
   - Page-aware context — knows which page farmer is on

9. OFFLINE-FIRST PWA
   - Serwist service worker for intelligent caching
   - IndexedDB write queue for offline actions
   - Idempotent replay with client UUIDs preventing duplicates
   - Offline photo uploads queue and process when online
   - PWA install prompt for Android and iOS

10. 8-LANGUAGE SYSTEM (i18n + RTL)
    - Database-stored translations (admin-editable via Neon dashboard)
    - URL slugs: /ur/dashboard, /pa/farms, /sd/prices
    - RTL mirroring for Urdu and Pashto with Nastaliq typography
    - Language switcher in nav everywhere

11. DIGITAL FARM RECORDS
    - Farm profiles with interactive map drawing (Leaflet)
    - GPS coordinates via geocoding
    - 7 record types: Sowing, Plant, Irrigation, Fertilizer, Pesticide,
      Disease, Harvest
    - Season tracking with weather snapshots
    - Yield and cost tracking per record

12. NOTIFICATIONS CENTER
    - Bell icon with unread count
    - Price alerts, weather alerts, pest alerts
    - Notification history

 SECTION 2: WHO IT'S FOR
================================================================================

PRIMARY USERS — Smallholder Farmers
------------------------------------
- 40 million+ farming households in Pakistan
- Average farm size: 0.5 to 5 acres
- Average annual income: Rs. 150,000 to 300,000
- Phone: Android budget smartphone, intermittent connectivity
- Literacy: Low — need simple navigation, local language, visual UI
- Pain points:
  * Cannot afford Rs. 2000-5000 per agronomist visit
  * No digital tools for farm management
  * Exploited by middlemen on crop prices
  * Cannot access government schemes
  * Reactive to weather/pests — no early warning system
  * No financial records — cannot get bank loans

SECONDARY USERS — Agricultural Officers & Extension Workers
------------------------------------------------------------
- Government extension officers managing 500+ farmers each
- Need dashboard views, farm-level summaries
- Bulk advisory capability for community outreach
- Track farmer progress across districts

TERTIARY USERS — Agri-Businesses & Policy Makers
-------------------------------------------------
- Seed companies, fertilizer suppliers, banks
- Need anonymized aggregate data for:
  * Crop forecasting
  * Loan underwriting
  * Supply chain planning
  * Government agricultural policy

 SECTION 4: INNOVATION & DIFFERENTIATION
================================================================================

What Makes Agropioo Different:

1. MULTI-AGENT AI ARCHITECTURE
   Most agricultural apps use a single chatbot. Agropioo uses 5
   specialized AI agents that collaborate — giving deeper, more
   accurate answers for specific farming topics.

2. LANGUAGE-FIRST (NOT LANGUAGE-ADDED)
   Most platforms add Urdu as an afterthought. Agropioo was built
   language-first with 8 languages as first-class features:
   database-stored translations, RTL layouts, Nastaliq typography.

3. RAG WITH DOMAIN-SPECIFIC KNOWLEDGE
   The AI doesn't hallucinate farming advice. It retrieves from 21
   curated documents covering Pakistan-specific crops, practices,
   fertilizers, pesticides, and government schemes.

4. OFFLINE-FIRST FOR REAL CONNECTIVITY
   Farmers in rural Pakistan have 2G connections. Agropioo's PWA
   with IndexedDB queue means the app works in airplane mode —
   records queue locally and sync when signal returns.

5. GROWTH-STAGE-AWARE WEATHER ADVISORIES
   Generic weather apps say "rain tomorrow." Agropioo says "rain
   tomorrow — but your wheat is at tillering stage, so delay
   irrigation by 2 days." Context-aware advice.

6. CROP-LEVEL PRICE PREDICTION WITH SELL/HOLD
   Not just showing prices — telling farmers what to do with them.
   14-day forecasts with actionable recommendations.

7. ZERO DEPENDENCY ON HARDWARE
   No IoT sensors, no drones, no satellite subscriptions. Everything
   works through the farmer's existing smartphone via APIs.
